### Career 1 — the ending screen (D, for career 1)

Result: **Round 4, Pick 7 — #103 overall. Final OVR 83. 4 seasons at UTSA. Bowl Win.**
Seasons: FR 7-5/60, SO 8-4/74 (+14), JR 8-4/74, SR 8-4/81 (+7), `ACROSS 4 SEASONS 60 → 81 +21`.
MOMENTS: S1 Carried the team through bowl season; S2 Won the rivalry game + bowl season; S3 **"Voted
captain without campaigning for it"**; S4 "Senior Day did not go to script" + bowl season.

**D1 — the loudest thing** is `Round 4, Pick 7`, in huge yellow display type, near the top. Nothing
else is close. The draft slot is the headline of my life.

**D2 — did it agree?** With my *football*, yes. With the career I actually played, no. I was voted
captain, stayed at one school four years through a coaching change, turned down money, ate last, and
coached up the kid trying to take my job — and the summary of that is a number that describes how
fast I am.

**D3 — did it tell me anything I did not already know? Yes, and this is the most interesting thing
on the screen.** The **MOMENTS** list surfaced things the game never showed me while I was playing:
> ⭐ *"Voted captain without campaigning for it"*
That line is the acknowledgement I spent two seasons waiting for, **and it was being held in escrow
until after I finished.** Same for "Senior Day did not go to script." The game *was* watching. It
just declined to tell me until the credits. The repeat is **welcome in itself and infuriating in its
timing** — showing me at the end that it noticed is proof it could have told me at the time.

**And then the thing that decides this whole playtest.** The ending screen prints the ladder again,
full height, and puts a label on my rung:
> `the staff trusts you`
> **`YOU ENDED HERE`**
**I ended on the rung I started on.** The character-creation screen put me at "the staff trusts you"
and four years of deliberate, consistent, sometimes costly good-teammate play ended me at "the staff
trusts you." **That is, word for word, the complaint the brief says was supposed to have been fixed.**
The five-rung ladder makes it *more* visible than before, not less: the game now draws me a picture
of the four rungs I did not move to.

**D4 — did anything make me want to run it back differently?** Yes — one thing, and not a good one.
Seeing `YOU ENDED HERE` next to my starting rung made me want to replay **to find out whether that
readout is reachable at all**, not to have a different career. That is a debugging impulse, not a
storytelling one.

**Two credits is all I ever banked** ("Up Forty at Halftime" twice, "The Team Banquet" once) against
charges that fired far more readily and from cards I could not always see coming — including one
with **no choice on it at all**.

Also worth saying: **MOMENTS in career 3 opened with *"A year with nothing to put on this list."***
for a freshman season in which I got up and finished a drive on a leg that put me in a boot until
January. Harsh, and another instance of the game declining to count the costly right thing.

## FINAL ANSWERS

### The one question, in my own words
**There is unmistakably something you are meant to be climbing, it is drawn for you clearly and
permanently, and in three careers I never climbed a single step of it.** The ladder is a good idea,
well presented, attached to a system that does not move. The game has *taught me it can do this
well* — `ON THE FIELD` climbed and fell through three named rungs in every career, promptly and
legibly, and the season rail prints a proud `ACROSS 4 SEASONS 48 → 77 +29`. The program ladder gets
none of that. **The fix I would want is not more visibility. It is for the thing to move, and for
the good news to get the treatment the bad news already gets.**

### (i) and (ii), as two separate sentences
**(i)** I could see exactly where I stood at every single moment, in the same place on every screen,
with all five rungs named so I always knew what better and worse looked like — **discoverability is
solved, completely.**
**(ii)** Where I stood changed **once in three careers, downward, in career 2's freshman year** — it
never once moved up, including after being voted captain, holding a locker room together through a
coaching change, and two explicit "Earned you credit with the program" lines.

### Report-back questions
- **Bored/confused/lost:** bored around **decision 22-25 of career 1**, when the captain vote failed
  to move anything; that is also when I started skimming. Confused at the season-2 Transfer Portal
  ("the two-deep is thin" on the *tougher competition* option) and twice on "Targeting Review".
- **Arbitrary choices:** yes, and a specific family of them — "The First One" / "No Longer New" /
  "You Start Counting" / "Saturday Night" all offer two flavour options with **no basis to pick**,
  and at least one of them (No Longer New) hides a standing charge behind that coin flip.
- **Invisible that should be visible:** how close I am to the next rung. **Prominent that did not
  deserve it:** the draft slot, which is the loudest thing on both ending screens in a game whose
  best material is the Moments list two screens down.
- **Made me want to play again:** the Moments list, and the writing on the best cards ("written in
  pencil", "because it is", the team banquet). **Made me want to stop:** `YOU ENDED HERE` next to a
  rung I never moved off.
- **The single thing most in the way of me playing one more career:**
  **I no longer believe the ladder can go up, so the half of the game I most want to play has no
  win condition I can reach.**

### Would I play a fourth?
**No.** I got my answer. A fourth career would be the same three cards in a different order, and I
already know what the ending screen will say about where I stand.
